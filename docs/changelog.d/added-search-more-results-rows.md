- **knowledge graph:** `search_shared_memory` in its `lean` and `compact` tiers lists the results after the three digests when the caller passes `limit` above 3. The digest showed three results whatever `limit` asked for, so the one route to a fourth was the `full` tier, which serializes every record. The further results arrive under `more_results`, one row each (`discovery_id`, `type`, `status`, a summary bounded at 100 characters), up to 17 rows inside a 2,000-byte budget of their own, with `state_summary.more_results_shown` counting them. A search that passes no `limit`, or a `limit` of 3 or less, is byte-identical to before and keeps its 3 KB budget; the `full` tier and `knowledge(action="search")` are unchanged. Rows are added only when all three digests survived the digest budget. The interface contract moves 1.32.0 → 1.33.0 to record the output change; no input digest moves. Not covered: `more_results` rows carry no writer attribution, tags or staleness field, so a reader still opens a row to learn who wrote it.
