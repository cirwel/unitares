- **identity / host observations:** the agent's own answer about a compaction can now be recorded beside its outcome. `POST /v1/runtime/observe` accepts `observation_kind="context_compaction_probe"` on the same identity-bound terms as the other kinds. It carries `probe_answer` (`same_process`, `new_process`, `cannot_tell`, or `no_answer` when the turn ended without one), `probe_version`, `guidance_present`, and the compaction it answers (`compaction_agent_uuid`, `compaction_observed_at`). An answer is stored as `agent_report`; `no_answer` is the hook's observation. Neither writes EISV or refreshes the session, and both stay out of the runtime-activity read model. `scripts/diagnostics/compaction_outcomes.py` now shows each answer beside its outcome. It counts consistency (`same_process` with `kept`, `new_process` with `minted`) and how many answers came from an identity other than the compacted one. Consistency is not accuracy. The question is asked with the compaction guidance in context, and `kept` is mostly produced by the Stop hook's own check-in, so the informative cells are answers beside `minted` and answers other than `same_process`. The plugin asks only when a session opts in, in a companion plugin PR. Built at the operator's direction on 2026-10-22, following the compaction decision (#3003) and record (#3004).
