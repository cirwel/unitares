- **dialectic:** a thesis no longer comes back as a failure, with `request_review` saying it was not recorded, when the spawned independent reviewer claims the slot and then exits non-zero inside the fast-crash wait, or when the dispatch call times out after the reviewer was spawned. The in-process fallback's facilitation request is refused in both cases because the session moved on; the handler now re-reads the session and reports the recorded thesis with the reviewer and phase it finds. If that reviewer already committed a terminal failure, or a decision whose durable effect is not confirmed applied, the call reports it as unsuccessful with the same failure fields and reconciliation guidance as the synthetic-timeout path, still with `thesis_recorded: true`. When the reviewer this call spawned exited inside the fast-crash wait and the session is still live, the call reports `spawned_reviewer_exited: true`: the slot holder may be that reviewer, so `request_review` gives the next move as unknown and points to reassignment or facilitation instead of naming the holder. If the re-read shows no other writer, the call reports `state_reload_required` with `thesis_recorded: true` instead. Nothing is dispatched twice and no session state is overwritten (#2905).
