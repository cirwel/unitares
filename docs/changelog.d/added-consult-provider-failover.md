- **inference:** a strong-model host whose provider account is at its usage limit, or whose CLI is logged out, now takes itself out of rotation and comes back on its own. A delegated call that fails that way (the provider ran and said so; our own orchestrator rejecting a spawn does not count) starts a cooldown for that host: until the provider's stated reset time when it gives one (Codex: "try again at Sep 29th, 2026 9:10 PM"; Claude's epoch and "resets 3pm" forms), capped at 12 hours so a limit lifted early is noticed, otherwise an exponential backoff from 30 minutes to 6 hours. An error that is not recognisably a quota or auth failure never starts one. While a host cools down, `host_adapter_available` reports it unavailable, `list_inference_hosts` shows the reason and retry time under `cooldown`, and `delegate_inference` to it is refused before any spawn with the retry time. A thorough `consult` whose chosen peer fails that way, with nothing left running, tries the next eligible peer from a different family in the same call while a full attempt still fits in the tool timeout; the response lists each hop under `failover`, and the route and audit record name the host that actually answered. Cooldowns are per process: a restart forgets them and costs at most one failed attempt, which the failover routes around.
