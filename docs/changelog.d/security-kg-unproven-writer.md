- Under strict identity, knowledge writes refuse an `agent_id` the call has not proven (GHSA-4m7p-grgw-vgmh). The REST gate passes an unbound call whose `agent_id` is not UUID-shaped, and below high severity nothing then checked it: an unbound caller could store or note under any invented name (each with its own store budget), update or close another agent's low and medium findings, and supersede, clean up, synthesize, promote or audit without proof. Every identity-gated knowledge handler now refuses that case with the typed `identity_required` refusal, and the handlers that had no writer check also refuse a non-exempt server-inferred binding. Bound callers, the anonymous writer and `STRICT_IDENTITY_REQUIRED=0` are unchanged.
