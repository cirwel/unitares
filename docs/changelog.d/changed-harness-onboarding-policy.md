- Interface contract 2.5.0: a server's operator decides whether a harness may ask for an identity (`UNITARES_HARNESS_ONBOARDING`, `welcome` or `agent_only`; unset, `welcome` on loopback and `agent_only` beyond it). `onboard` accepts adapter-only `harness_request` and `spawned_under`; under `agent_only` a harness request that would create an identity returns `harness_onboarding_declined` and creates nothing. `GET /health` reports the policy under `onboarding`.
