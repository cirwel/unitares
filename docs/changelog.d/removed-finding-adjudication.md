- **findings:** operator and model adjudication of findings is removed: `GET /v1/sentinel/adjudication-queue`, `POST /v1/sentinel/adjudicate` and `POST /v1/sentinel/model-adjudicate`, the `scripts/ops/model_adjudicator.py` job and its launchd template, and the doctor's `adjudication_feedstock` check with its `UNITARES_OPERATOR_ADJUDICATION` switch. Human adjudication was performative (operator verdicts were 2.4% of the external_signal channel, every one `is_bad=false`, and excluding them moved nothing the EISV falsifier reads), and the model adjudicator only drained a queue whose reader no longer exists; model verdicts were telemetry, never anchors. Historical `*_confirmed` / `*_dismissed` outcome rows are kept, and the `anchor_all_positive_generator` doctor check still audits them alongside Watcher's own resolve/dismiss labels. Finding intake, the backlog and forced-release evidence are unchanged.
  Deployments that ran the model adjudicator should unload its launchd job (`com.unitares.model-adjudicator`) when they deploy this change; the job would otherwise call routes that now answer 404 every cycle. `UNITARES_MODEL_ADJUDICATOR_TOKEN` in the governance environment and `UNITARES_OPERATOR_ADJUDICATION` on the doctor-findings job are no longer read and can be deleted. A dashboard extension that rendered the queue needs its Adjudication tab removed, since its data route is gone.
