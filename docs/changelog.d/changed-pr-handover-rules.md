- **PR workflow:** Auth and identity-enforcement PRs are no longer held as drafts for the operator: the two-family `review` check gates them and their owner readies and labels them. On a `second_family_paths` diff every committer comes from one model family, and a successor from another family takes delivery only. A direct operator instruction to handle, land, finish or take over a named PR grants branch-writing, readiness and queue entry together; waivers and deployments are still asked separately (operator ruling, 2026-11-20).
