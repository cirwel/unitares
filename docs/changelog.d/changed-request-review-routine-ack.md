- **agent response envelopes:** a routine `request_review` acknowledgement no longer repeats its canonical payload under `raw_governance`, applying the #2437 rule (routine responses say each fact once; anything abnormal keeps its full explanation). Routine means the review opened and now waits on a known move: the thesis recorded and the reviewer slot open, assigned or filled by a dispatched reviewer process, or (with `use_brief_as_thesis=false`) the caller's thesis owed, and every key in the payload is on an allowlist of those shapes. Such an ack carries `response_shape: "routine"`, keeps `session_id`, `phase`, `whose_move`, `one_call_review`, `thesis_source`, `orchestrated_review` and `reviewer_dispatch` in `state_summary`, takes `agent_uuid` and a compact `written_as` from the agent signature, and its `raw_governance_hint` names `dialectic(action="get", session_id=...)`, which returns the session as it stands later rather than the ack, so `raw_governance_available` is not set. An ack that carries the review itself (a verdict or synthesis, recusal, a facilitation request, a reviewer-host limit, coerced arguments, a terminal phase, a missing session id, or any key nobody has classified) keeps the payload, with `response_shape: "full"` and a `response_shape_reason` naming what kept it. A thesis that was not recorded still returns `success: false` and passes through whole. Measured on fixture acks (2026-10-19): 2,888 B to 895 B with a server-inferred signature, 1,562 B to 896 B with a caller-proven one; the live reading in #2443 was about 3.0 KB. The dialectic canary reads the lifted `state_summary` when `raw_governance` is absent. The canonical `dialectic` tool is unchanged, and no input schema changes (#2443).
