- **attestation docs:** `EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS.md`, `SCOPE_AND_THREAT_MODEL.md` and the `drr.v1` decision packet no longer describe resolution attestations as HMAC keyed on each agent's `api_key` as if the deployment used it. That party-HMAC scheme is in the code, but as of 2026-09-25 the maintainer deployment has not issued a party signature since 2026-06-24 UTC, the date of the most recent resolved dialectic record carrying one. `EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS.md` also states more precisely what was shelved on 2026-04-19: party-held asymmetric (DPoP-style) keys, which party-level non-repudiation would need, and that remains shelved; server-side signing was not part of that decision. Server-side Ed25519 signing exists: as of 2026-09-25 the maintainer deployment's lease attestations use it, and the dialectic resolution receipt (`drr.v1`) is wired, dormant and has issued no receipts. A receipt would attest the deployment's stored record, not either party's intent. The consequence is unchanged: a record from this system still cannot be verified by an operator who does not already trust its issuer, and nothing here enables or proposes enabling `drr.v1`. The dormant-capability registry's `drr.v1` row now lists a second principal as its own precondition, alongside custody, a pinning channel, key history and a revocation or transparency policy, and the packet's summary names all five instead of two.
