- **effect binding:** an effect grant's lifetime is now capped at 300 seconds. `mint_effect_grant` clamps a requested `ttl_seconds` into [5, 300], so `/v1/effect-grant` issues a shorter grant to a caller that asks for more, and `verify_effect_grant` refuses a grant whose `exp - iat` exceeds 300 seconds, whose `iat` is more than 30 seconds ahead of now, or that carries no `iat` (reason `bad_lifetime`). In-tree producers ask for 30 to 120 seconds and are unaffected. After a nonce is consumed, `/v1/effect-veto` now purges `effects.consumed_nonces` at most once a minute, in batches of up to 1000 rows, as migration 054 described. It deletes only rows whose grants verify would already refuse: those that expired more than 60 seconds ago, and those whose `grant_exp` lies further ahead than any grant the server mints could reach. A purge failure is logged and does not change the veto outcome. No migration is needed.
