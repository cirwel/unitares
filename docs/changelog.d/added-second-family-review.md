- **review gate:** a diff that touches a security-sensitive path now needs a passing full review from two different model families before the `review` check goes green. The paths live in `scripts/dev/review_policy.json`: the OAuth provider, identity and auth modules and schema, the strict-identity gates, the MCP authorization gate and its wiring, lease and effect authorization, the strong-model host adapter and Antigravity client, the dialectic reviewer's backends, and the review gate, its policy and its workflow; `_boundary` in the file names what is deliberately left off. Families are OpenAI (Codex, native or local), Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Antigravity); a recorded reviewer counts only when its name carries its family as a word, and a fix-verification receipt never counts. CI enforces the rule, reading the policy as merged on the pull request's base ref, never from its head, and requires the second family if the changed paths cannot be read. `review.sh` does not start the second review itself: on a sensitive diff with one family it exits UNREVIEWED and prints the exact `--fresh --reviewer` commands for eligible providers, so the round cap and the scheduled sweep are unchanged. The motivating cases: on PR #2486 the first reviewer returned a bare CLEAN and a second family then found two P2 defects, and the Antigravity isolation hole (standing permission grants loaded from `~/.gemini`) was caught by one family's review.
