- **ops:** `sentinel-plist.py` handles a governance LaunchAgent that keeps no bearer in its plist, because its launcher loads `UNITARES_HTTP_API_TOKEN` from a store outside it (an OS keychain, say) before exec'ing the server. Before, `check` refused every such host with "has no usable EnvironmentVariables:UNITARES_HTTP_API_TOKEN", so `deploy-sentinel.sh` stopped at preflight, and `deploy-apply.sh` then held every other service sharing that checkout. When the governance plist names neither the key nor the secrets wrapper, `check` and `probe` now send the Sentinel's bearer to the running service over the per-user socket on the read-only, bearer-gated `GET /v1/tools`, and refuse on a 401, on any other non-200, or when the service cannot be reached. `render` refuses an unwrapped Sentinel in that case, because there is no value to copy. A governance plist that has the key but an empty or placeholder value is still refused, as before.
