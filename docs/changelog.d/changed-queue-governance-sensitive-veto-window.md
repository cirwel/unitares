- **Merge queue:** A governance-sensitive PR is no longer left for a hand merge. Following the operator ruling of 2026-11-16, `scripts/ops/pr-babysitter.sh` arms one once its `review` check has passed with two model families, its body states the effect on pause and verdict rates on an `Expected effect:` line, and 24 hours have passed since its `approved-to-merge` label went on; removing the label in that time vetoes it. A waiting PR does not hold the queue. Diffs touching the sensitivity manifest, its checker or its CI workflow stay hand-merge only, unreadable inputs fail closed to a hand merge, and `PR_QUEUE_SENSITIVE_DELEGATED=0` restores the old rule. `scripts/dev/review_policy.json` now lists every manifest file under `second_family_paths`, so those diffs need two-family review. The veto window is a convention among cooperating agents, not an enforced boundary.
