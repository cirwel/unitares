- **dashboard:** a new core **Timeline** tab draws one row per agent across the last 1, 6 or 24 hours from `GET /v1/timeline`. Marks: check-ins (shape by policy action; hook-recorded ones hollow, with a toggle to hide them), graded outcomes (✓/✕), knowledge writes, declared lineage (the child's row sits under its parent, labelled subagent, successor or continuation from its `spawn_reason`, as `src/identity/lineage_semantics.py` classifies it), lifecycle changes, and bands for pauses and dialectic reviews. Selecting a mark shows its record in UTC: for a check-in, its report excerpt when the server returns one (otherwise it says the text was not retained); for an outcome, the confidence it grades against the result. Rows are ordered by most recent event, capped at 25 until "show all", with a filter on label or id; kinds the server truncated are named. The 1h and 6h windows refresh on the live tick; the 24h window loads on demand.
