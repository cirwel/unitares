- **review gate:** a verdict with no reasoning is no longer recorded as a review. Antigravity twice replied with nothing but `VERDICT: CLEAN` on PR #2486: once after reading about 97,000 tokens of the diff and changed files, and once on a five-line fix. After the first, an independent Claude review of the same diff found two P2 and two P3 defects, so a bare CLEAN was hiding real problems, not summarising a clean change. A verdict now needs at least 120 non-whitespace characters (about 25 words) of reasoning before the `VERDICT` line; that floor is a judgement call, set low on purpose, because it exists to catch an empty verdict, not to grade a short one (200 was tried first and rejected a terse but genuine review). When Antigravity replies with a bare verdict, the conversation is resumed once and asked what it examined; a local reviewer that stays bare is recorded as a failed attempt, so the next reviewer runs instead; and `review.sh record` refuses a bare verdict outright. Every reviewer prompt now says so. Native Codex review is unaffected: its records are built directly from GitHub's review, not parsed from reviewer text.
