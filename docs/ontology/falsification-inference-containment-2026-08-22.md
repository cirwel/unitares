# Falsification inference containment — 2026-08-22

**Status:** initial correction for the five EISV falsification claims listed
below. The systemic follow-up in
[`falsification-design-system-audit-2026-08-23.md`](falsification-design-system-audit-2026-08-23.md)
audits all 45 ledger rows and the repeated-read protocol. Historical reports
remain preserved as provenance; their former categorical interpretations do not
remain live conclusions.

This correction separates four questions that earlier surfaces conflated:

1. Did the code path consume the source we thought it consumed?
2. Did the instrument identify the causal or predictive estimand?
3. Did the observed sample have independent units and adequate power?
4. Did the evidence cross a decision standard fixed before the read?

Only a test that answers all applicable questions may earn `REFUTED`. An
invalid or endogenous design does not become a negative result. It becomes
`UNIDENTIFIED` or `WITHDRAWN`.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `EARNED` | A pre-registered test of the stated estimand passed its predeclared standard. |
| `REFUTED` | A pre-registered, adequately powered test of the stated estimand failed its predeclared standard. |
| `BENCHMARK FAIL` | An exact registered estimator or mechanism missed its benchmark; transfer to a deployed capability requires separate identification. |
| `IMPLEMENTATION MISMATCH` | Source, configuration, or algebra contradicts an exact description of the deployed mechanism. |
| `PATH BOUND` | Source or algebra excludes a named direct path; broader causal or dynamic claims remain open. |
| `DEPLOYMENT SNAPSHOT` | A dated census describes the deployment without supplying independent trials or a general result. |
| `INCONCLUSIVE` | A usable test ran but its interval, support, or decision standard did not resolve the claim. |
| `UNIDENTIFIED` | The design cannot estimate the target claim, usually because predictor and outcome are endogenous, the counterfactual is absent, or the unit of analysis is wrong. |
| `WITHDRAWN` | A previously reported inference was invalidated by a scope, provenance, dependence, or instrument defect. Historical numbers may remain only with that status. |
| `UNTESTED AS DEPLOYED` | A test returned a negative, but its instrument had no usable power against the deployed claim. |
| `LABEL-BLOCKED` | The external-label supply cannot yet support the registered test. |

`REFUTED BY CONSTRUCTION` is retired as an active scientific status. Direct
source proofs now use `IMPLEMENTATION MISMATCH` or `PATH BOUND`. Endogeneity, a
missing direct edge, or absence of observed threshold crossings is not a
refutation.

## Reclassified claims

### 1. Historical outcome/AUC inference

**Former claim:** adding EISV/prior-state features makes prospective prediction
worse than a previous-outcome baseline.

**Status:** `WITHDRAWN FOR TARGET INFERENCE`; the registered question is
unresolved pending the single 2026-12-01 trusted-anchor read.

The historical read used the contaminated `--anchor-scope all` cohort. Its 101
"clusters" were `(agent, prior-state snapshot)` permutation blocks, not 101
independent adjudicated failures. The corrected stop-rule contract already
withdraws the inference and fixes trusted scope, a frozen cutoff, harness
exclusion, the selection-aware null, and the reporting tuple. The recurring
local trend was paused on 2026-08-22 because its TSV did not record anchor scope
and would have mixed the historical all-scope rows with the current trusted
default.

The frozen 2026-08-09 trusted matrix remains a dated descriptive read: no
candidate cleared the selection-aware null in that small cohort. It is not
evidence that EISV is harmful, refuted, or incapable of future lift.

### 2. Basin-flip/intervention regression

**Former claim:** the regressor is an arithmetic summand of the regressand with
a known coefficient, making the failed regression a structural negative result.

**Status:** `UNIDENTIFIED` for the empirical association or causal claim.

Regime instability enters behavioral S with mixture weight 0.35. S then enters
a nonlinear, thresholded, stateful policy pipeline that may emit an
intervention event. The event is not a numeric regressand containing the flip
count as an arithmetic summand, and 0.35 is neither a regression coefficient nor
a causal effect size. Because both variables are generated by the same
endogenous observer, their correlation cannot validate external predictive or
causal usefulness.

Two narrower findings survive:

- the source topology contains a self-referential path; and
- 936 of roughly 3,001 measured regime changes (31%) were manufactured by the
  documented storage-coercion defect.

Those are mechanism and data-quality findings, not a negative empirical result
about EISV.

### 3. Guide/decision-E basin trap

**Former claim:** the guide self-loop cannot flip an agent across `E >= 0.6` and
hold it there, refuted with adequate power.

**Status:** `PATH BOUND` for a direct same-check-in path;
`UNIDENTIFIED` for the recursive dynamic counterfactual.

The source guard correctly proves that the verdict classifier is handed the ODE
`GovernanceState`, rather than behavioral E directly. The descriptive telemetry
read found no observed complete-case BOUNDARY row whose ODE E failed the 0.6
conjunct. That excludes the proposed direct edge in the observed deployment.

It does not exclude a dynamic edge. Behavioral EISV spring-couples into the ODE
by default through `k_anchor=0.1`, so a decision-history perturbation can reach a
later classifier state. The existing read holds the realized history fixed and
does not recursively replay ODE, EMA, policy, outcomes, or future decisions
under a neutralized decision channel. Repeated telemetry rows are not
independent counterfactual units.

The original query also admitted incomplete classifier inputs. In the dated
2026-08-22 audit, 2,124 of 13,772 BOUNDARY rows (15.4%) had missing coherence;
SQL three-valued logic sent those rows to the `ELSE 'boundary'` branch. The
analysis now reports input completeness and excludes incomplete rows before
recomputing the basin.

### 4. Distributional signal probe

**Former claim:** static EISV dispersion carried no predictive lift, proving the
bottleneck was observation rather than representation and killing
distributional EISV for signal.

**Status:** `INCONCLUSIVE` for the representation or bottleneck claim;
`NOT GREENLIT` remains the valid operational decision.

The objective strict scope had zero negative outcomes and could not exercise the
probe. The task-scope result used weaker, partly self-reported labels, predated
the trusted-anchor and selection-aware-null corrections, and treated repeated
rows as if the point estimate alone decided the question. Static recent-state
dispersion was also only a proxy for the proposed precision-weighted update
rule. The negative point estimate therefore did not identify whether the
bottleneck was observation, representation, or the proxy itself.

The conservative decision not to build a larger dynamics change *on that
evidence* remains sound. What is withdrawn is the stronger `KILL` and bottleneck
inference. A future, separately preregistered test may revisit the capability;
the 2026-06-22 result does not forbid it.

### 5. Runtime EMA reference benchmark

**Former claim:** individuality-v2 Leg C refuted the usefulness of the deployed
per-agent EMA reference.

**Status:** `BENCHMARK FAIL` for the frozen cold-start reconstruction benchmark;
`UNIDENTIFIED` for the already-warm deployed EMA.

Leg C pre-registered a useful bounded comparison and the tested reconstruction
lost to last-value persistence in every eligible stream. But the instrument
cold-started a runtime-form mirror at the registration boundary because the
fresh-data rule excluded the reference's earlier history. It therefore did not
replay the deployed, already-warm EMA state. Three of seven nominal agents also
replicated one Raspberry Pi, and the pooled cross-dimension binomial treated
dependent trials as independent.

The exact cold-start reconstruction failed its registered benchmark. That does
not identify whether the warmed deployed reference helps, harms, or merely needs
retuning. The individuality axiom remains `UNTESTED AS DEPLOYED`; Leg C never
gated that axiom.

## Reopening requirements

Outcome grounding reopens only through the registered trusted-anchor read or a
materially new, separately pre-registered premise. Do not resume the historical
weekly trend or append a new scope to its TSV.

The dynamic basin question requires a matched recursive replay or prospective
shadow that advances the full ODE, behavioral EMA, policy, and outcome history
with and without the decision-history channel. Predeclare the counterfactual,
the horizon, the threshold event, the independent unit (agent or episode), the
input schedule, and the decision standard. Until then, report the direct-path
guard and the observed margin separately from the untested dynamic effect.

The deployed-reference question requires either a replay initialized from the
persisted pre-window EMA state or a prospective comparison that records both
the live reference and a frozen baseline. The independent unit must account for
replicated processes on one host. A fresh cold-start reconstruction cannot close
the warmed-reference claim.

Downstream documents, paper ledgers, scheduled reports, and shared-memory
entries must link to this correction or carry the corrected status inline.

`DEPLOYMENT SNAPSHOT` into `REFUTED` or “negative result.”
