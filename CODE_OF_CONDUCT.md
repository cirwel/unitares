# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

UNITARES welcomes technical disagreement, skeptical review, negative results,
and good-faith challenges to its claims. Participation must remain respectful,
specific, and safe for the people involved.

Expected behavior includes:

- criticizing claims, evidence, and implementations rather than people;
- stating uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- respecting privacy, attribution, and reasonable boundaries;
- making space for different backgrounds and levels of experience; and
- helping keep issues, reviews, and discussions focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, community spaces, and project representation in public settings.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. To request a private conduct
channel, open an issue containing only `contact requested — conduct`; the
maintainer will move the conversation off the public thread. Immediate threats
or platform abuse should also be reported through GitHub's safety tools.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close interactions, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

Enforcement decisions may be revisited when material context changes. No one,
including the maintainer, is exempt from this standard.
